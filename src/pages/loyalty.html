When Do You Need to Give Your Phone Number In-Store?

credit_card Manage Linked Cards

Once you tap your physical debit/credit card or Apple Pay / Google Pay with your phone number once at our Fitzroy counter, Square automatically links that card to your Loyalty profile so every future tap earns Beans automatically. You only need to give us your phone number in-store in three situations:

add_card 1. Using a New Card

If you are paying with a new physical card, a replacement card, or a different Apple Pay / Google Pay device for the first time.

payments 2. Paying with Cash

When paying with cash at the counter, let our barista know your mobile number so your Beans are credited to your account.

redeem 3. Claiming a Reward

When you have 50+ Beans and want to redeem your Free Specialty Coffee, give your mobile number at the register before paying.
